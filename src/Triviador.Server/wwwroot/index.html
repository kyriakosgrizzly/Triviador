How to play
  1. Castles. Each player places a castle (3 hit points).
  2. Expansion. Everyone guesses a number. Closest claims 2 territories, runner-up claims 1. Repeat until the map is full.
  3. Battle. Take turns attacking a neighbouring enemy territory. You and the defender get the same question; if you both get it right (or both wrong), a closest-guess tiebreak decides.
  4. Castles fall after 3 successful attacks, and their owner's land goes to the attacker.
  5. Most land value after 4 battle rounds wins.
