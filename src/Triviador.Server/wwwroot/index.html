    How to play
    1. Match the pile. Play a card of the same colour (Geography, Science, History, Tech), the same number or the same action — or a wild.
    2. Answer to play it. Every card is a question. You get a 3-second head start; right lands the card, wrong spends it and you draw 1.
    3. Steal. After 3 seconds anyone can answer. A right steal lets you drop a card from your biggest colour and take the turn; a wrong one draws 2.
    4. Can't play? Draw a card. If it fits, you can play it straight away.
    5. Action cards: Lockout (no steals next turn), Hot Seat (reverse, no head start), Pop Quiz (next player answers or draws 2), Pick Your Topic and Duel (wilds).
    6. First to empty their hand wins.

            Card guide
            • Number — match the category or the number.
            • 🔒 Lockout — nobody can steal the next player's question.
            • ⇄ Hot Seat — reverse; the next player gets no head start.
            • ?! Pop Quiz — next player answers first; wrong draws 2.
            • ◆ Pick Your Topic — wild: choose the category.
            • ⚔ Duel — wild: you vs an opponent, loser draws 4.
            • Wrong answer: card is spent, you draw 1. Nobody answers: card comes back, draw 1.
            • Right steal: you drop a number card from your biggest colour and take the turn. Wrong steal: draw 2.
            • Stealing: pick during the 3-second head start and your answer goes in the moment it ends.
            • Keys 1–4 answer, F full screen.
